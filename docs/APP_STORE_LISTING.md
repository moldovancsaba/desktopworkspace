## Description
Desktop Workspace is a lightweight, open-source macOS utility for organizing your apps into reusable desktop layouts.

- **Built-in Layouts:** 3 Columns, 2 Top + 1 Bottom, 1 Top + 2 Bottom, 2 Columns, 1 Left + 2 Right, 2 Left + 1 Right, 2×2 Grid, 3 Rows, and Full Screen.
- **Visual Slot Assignment:** Click any slot in the layout preview to assign an installed macOS app.
- **Full Screen Tiled or Framed Group Mode:** Tile apps edge-to-edge across your display, or group them inside a resizable container frame you can move, maximize, or dock to the sidebar.
- **Re-Align & Auto-Lock:** Snap all windows back to their slots anytime (`⌘⇧R`), reopen closed apps on Re-Align, or enable Auto-Lock Alignment.
- **Menu Bar Quick Launch:** Launch or re-align any saved workspace from the macOS menu bar.
- **100% Private & Local:** No accounts, no analytics, and no network connections. Workspaces are saved locally on your Mac.
