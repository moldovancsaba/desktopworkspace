## 3. macOS Accessibility Permission
Desktop Workspace requests macOS Accessibility permission (`AXIsProcessTrusted`) solely to:
- Position and resize application windows into your chosen layout.
- Trigger Mission Control to create and switch to a new Desktop Space when requested.

No keystrokes, screen pixels, or window contents are ever logged, recorded, or transmitted.
