# Privacy Policy — Desktop Workspace

**Effective Date:** November 18, 2026

Desktop Workspace respects your privacy and is designed to operate entirely on your Mac.

## Data Collection

- **No Personal Data Collected:** Desktop Workspace does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry.
- **No Network Connections:** The app does not communicate with external servers.

## Local Storage

- Your saved workspace configurations (workspace names, chosen layouts, and selected application paths/bundle identifiers) are stored locally on your Mac at:
  `~/Library/Application Support/DesktopWorkspace/workspaces.json`
- You can delete this data at any time by deleting workspaces in the app or removing that file.

## System Permissions

- **Accessibility:** Used solely when you launch a workspace to position and resize application windows into your chosen layout and optionally create a new Desktop Space via Mission Control. No keystrokes, screen contents, or window contents are recorded or transmitted.
